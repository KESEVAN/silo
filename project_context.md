This idea is the concept behind the series called Silo, behind Severance, behind The Circle, and any other science fiction films. The pattern recognized over here is compartmentalization or departmentalization of various components isolated from each other, yet cohesively interlinked or interconnected towards each other to achieve a common balance or proper functioning as a whole.
For example, taking the Silo series as an example, we have:
- the mechanical
- the judiciary
- the doctors
- health
- the farmers
- the porters who carry messages to different levels
Now, imagine translating this into an agent swarm. We will have a swarm of agents with different personalities, functions, and capabilities, provided we have a proper messaging system among them via the porter system. Each will have privileged-level access to different systems and components based on the levels, providing secure identity-based agents, which gives them role-based access control and implements authentication and zero-trust policy, ensuring that no agents are being trusted blindly.
This form of non-human identity implementation on multiple agents to achieve a common goal should be implemented somewhere in some domain, but that is not determined. What we would need is the very essence of having this system in place and to simulate what would be its outcome. Kind of like how silos are of 50 or hundreds, as stated in the series, how can this be translated into something which is, in reality, using the agentic systems we have now?
The main goal is to not make it too complex or ride the hype train of implementing all the different fancy trends and terminologies, and use what really makes sense. Even if it means using a normal RNN network, or even if it requires complex harness engineering or graph engineering, so be it. One must always think about what the best appropriate solution or a system is that can be implemented with good systems thinking and design
