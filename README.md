### ▓▓ THE PREMISE

No single mind runs this place. There is no god-process watching everything at once —
that idea was tried, and it failed the way centralized things always fail: one crack,
and the whole structure goes with it.

So instead — **levels**. Sealed. Independent. Each with its own purpose, its own
authority, its own blind spots. An agent on one level does not know what an agent
three floors down is doing, or *if* it exists at all. That's not a bug. That's the
point.

Nothing here trusts anything by default. Not a message, not a request, not even
a familiar name. Every voice has to prove who it is — every single time — before
anyone listens.

Each department speaks its own language and answers to its own rules. None of
them talk to each other directly. None of them are *allowed* to.

---

### ▓▓ THE PORTERS

Between every level walks something else — a courier, a go-between, trusted with
nothing but the act of carrying. The Porters don't make decisions. They don't
read what they deliver, not really. They just ask one question, over and over,
before anything moves an inch:

> *"Do you have the right to send this — and does the one below have the right
> to receive it?"*

Get it wrong, and the message simply never arrives. No warning. No appeal.
The silence itself is the answer.

---

### ▓▓ THE LAW

No identity is assumed. No access is inherited. No agent is trusted because it
was trusted yesterday.

Every level operates under a single unspoken rule, carried down from whoever
built this thing first:

> **Prove it, or you don't move.**
